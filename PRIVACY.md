# VoiceMapper privacy policy

_Last updated: 22 November 2026_

VoiceMapper is a voice-training app. It is fully local: **nothing you do in the app leaves your device.**

## What the app uses
- **Microphone.** Your voice is analysed live, on the device, to show pitch, resonance and voice-quality readings. Audio is never recorded to storage, uploaded or shared.
- **Face/fingerprint or device PIN** (only if you turn on App Lock). This is handled by your phone's operating system; VoiceMapper never sees your biometric data.

## What the app stores
Only on your device, in the app's private storage:
- summaries of your practice sessions (for example average pitch, time spent and grid positions, never audio),
- your baseline, goals, exercise results, strain-alert times and settings.

This data is excluded from cloud backups and device-to-device transfer.

## What the app does not do
- No accounts or sign-in.
- No internet access (the Android app does not even request the network permission).
- No analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.
- No data is sold, shared or sent to the developer or anyone else.

## Your control
- **Export:** Settings ▸ Export saves your data as a file to a place you choose.
- **Delete:** Settings ▸ Delete all data erases everything. Uninstalling the app also deletes it.

## Health note
VoiceMapper gives estimates to support practice. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose. If you have pain, lasting hoarseness or lose your voice, see a speech-language pathologist.
